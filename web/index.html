How Déjà View works

The launch moment is the first time a coin's trading reaches $5,000. The buyers are the 8 biggest buyers of $500 or more in the hour after it. For each buyer, Déjà View looks at their trades in the 30 days before the launch moment, and nothing after it.

Launch Reflex measures how often a buyer's earlier launch buys went on to offer a strong move within an hour of their buy, ranked against 37 reference launch buyers. In a test frozen before any results were seen, a buyer's score on older launches predicted their score on newer ones. It is historical context, not a profit score, and not a prediction.

Echoes are earlier launches that two or more of today's buyers were also early on. They show repeated behaviour. They do not show that the wallets are connected.

Characters are generated from each wallet address, so the same wallet always looks the same, in every launch.

Launch Reflex measures whether a buyer has repeatedly got into earlier launches that went on to offer strong first-hour moves. It is historical context, not a profit score, a win rate, or a prediction.
