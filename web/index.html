Déjà View

See who got in early, and whether they've done it before.

Déjà View checks a pump.fun launch's biggest first-hour buyers against their earlier launch buys, then finds past launches that looked the same.

Powered by Nansen API
Replay a past launch:

1Paste a launch

Paste a pump.fun token address. Buyers appear right away. Similar past launches appear once the coin is an hour past its launch moment, the first time it reached $5,000 of trading.

2Read the answer

The top box tells you whether buyers with a good launch record are in, how this launch's buying compares with usual, and what happened to launches that looked the same.

3Dig in

Check each strong buyer's earlier launches, open the wallet on Nansen, and compare the past launches, including the one that went the other way.

Déjà View shows evidence from past launches. It is not a forecast and it is not advice.
