# Demo recording: shot list and captions

**Prepared 2026-09-17.** Target length: 30 to 45 seconds, readable with the sound off.

## Before you press record

1. Open **http://localhost:8420** in Chrome, full screen, at 100% zoom. Hide the bookmarks bar.
2. Use the local site, not the Vercel site. Only the local site can run a live scan.
3. Copy the live coin address so you can paste it in one move:
   `G2KDX81e31T6pZbkrJLPKAXqKcd8VoE3UEp83rRtpump` ($HYPED, launched 2026-09-16 19:22 UTC).
4. Record the live scan as its own clip. It takes a few minutes, and you cut the wait in the edit.

## Why HYPED

- 487 buyers spent $500 or more in its first hour, from about 13,000 traders on the day.
- The 8 biggest spent $12k to $22k each, and they arrive spread from minute 3 to minute 20.
- Its first hour is complete, so the page narrates a full hour honestly.
- Rejected: STRATEGY, USDF, ECTF, FAIR, and ZCAT. Each had 2 big buyers with identical amounts,
  which looks like scripted trading. DONO and LOCKINU have fewer buyers, bunched late in the hour.

## Credits

- 647 credits were left after choosing the coin. Past live scans cost 312 to 511.
- HYPED's buyers trade a lot, so its scan may cost more than average.
- If a scan runs out part way, the data it already fetched stays saved, and a retake of the
  same coin costs only the missing parts.
