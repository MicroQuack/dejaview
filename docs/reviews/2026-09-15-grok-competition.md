### 4. Gaps that would stand out

Nansen asked for “an agent, game, trading tool, visualization, or something completely unexpected.” Public Meridian work so far is one terminal, one fish tank, one thesis desk, one history quiz, one chat bot.

Still empty, and past winners prove they like these:

- **A real game** (not a dashboard with sprites)
- **Something physical** on a desk
- **Hyperliquid perps** as the whole product
- **Prediction markets** vs onchain flows
- **Alert that fires, then trades** (the API can now quote and swap)
- **Robinhood stocks** beyond the aquarium tank
